# Temporary civilization backgrounds

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, then encoded as WebP (quality 84) for the game. Original generations remain in the Codex generated_images folder. These are atmospheric concept paintings, not archaeological reconstructions. No reference images used.

## Greece — greece/background-v1.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: temporary full-screen background artwork for a civilization chess web game, widescreen 16:9 landscape. Ancient Greece: weathered ivory Doric marble temple and columns at the left and right edges overlooking a calm Aegean bay, distant mountain silhouettes, olive branches near the edges. Tasteful painterly strategy-game environment, atmospheric depth, rich stone texture, teal-blue sea and muted warm gold evening sunlight. Calm open center with low detail and balanced mid-dark values so an opaque chessboard UI can sit over it. Architectural landmarks visible near the sides. No people, no chess pieces, no board, no lettering, no logos, no watermark. A standalone environment painting filling the frame, not a screenshot or UI mockup.

## Egypt — egypt/background-v1.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: temporary full-screen background artwork for a civilization chess web game, widescreen 16:9 landscape. Ancient Egypt: monumental sandstone temple pylons and an obelisk at the edges, a distant pyramid silhouette beyond the Nile, small palms near the riverbank. Tasteful painterly strategy-game environment, atmospheric depth, rich sandstone texture, warm sand and amber twilight, restrained lapis blue shadows and water. Calm open center with low detail and balanced mid-dark values so an opaque chessboard UI can sit over it. Architectural landmarks visible near the sides. No people, no chess pieces, no board, no readable lettering, no logos, no watermark. A standalone environment painting filling the frame, not a screenshot or UI mockup.
